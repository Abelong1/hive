About this archive

This archive collects thesis and capstone projects completed within the Computer Engineering program so future students, faculty, and panel members can quickly check what has already been researched before proposing new topics. Entries show titles, abstracts, methodology, and reference lists. Full manuscripts are available on request rather than posted openly, to protect the privacy of study participants and the department's records.

Maintained by

ACES (Association of Computer Engineering Students) officers, updated with each newly completed batch of theses.

Adding new entries

Entries are added by ACES officers after each researcher's consent to publish is confirmed. Researchers can submit or edit their own entry in the researcher portal.

Requesting a full manuscript

Reach the CPE Program Office at cpe.department@sti-caloocan.edu.ph with the thesis ID shown in its detail view.
